# Fantasy Armor 1.3.1

For all supported loaders on Minecraft 1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.19.3, 1.19.4, 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.10, and 1.21.11.

- Fixed armor dye removal in water cauldrons. Washing removes the color and consumes one water level while preserving the armor's other data.

Other Minecraft versions remain on Fantasy Armor 1.3.
